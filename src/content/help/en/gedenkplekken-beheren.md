## Creating a new memorial

Go to **Memorials** and create a new memorial. You follow a short wizard:

1. Details of the deceased (name, dates, photo).
2. A web address for the memorial.
3. The plan, and with it the period.

The family then gets access and the memorial can fill up with photos, videos and memories.

## Active or archived

Every memorial has a period:

- **Active**: the memorial is accessible and fully usable.
- **Archived**: the period has ended. The memorial is no longer visible, but the data is kept for another 6 months.

## Extending and reactivating

- You can extend an active memorial at any time, from 1 month, via the settings of that memorial.
- You reactivate an archived memorial by extending it by at least 3 months. Everything is then available again straight away.
- If an archived memorial isn't extended within 6 months, all data is permanently deleted. Managers receive warning emails in good time.

## Access to the memorial

Memorials are private. Visitors request access via the share link or a QR code; the managers chosen by the family approve or decline requests.

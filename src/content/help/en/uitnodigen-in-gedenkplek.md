## Who can invite?

Managers give access directly. Visitors can **suggest** someone; a manager then has to approve.

## Inviting someone as a manager

Go to **Invite** (or **Settings → Invite**) and enter the email address. The person receives an email with a link to join the memorial.

## Via QR code or share link

Share the **QR code** or the **share link**. Whoever scans or clicks enters their email address, name and relationship to the deceased, and that's how they request access.

## Approving requests

As a manager you get a notification. Open the right memorial and approve or decline the request. Once approved, the person automatically has access.

## Does the person already have an account?

If someone already has a Remoria account, they simply log in with their usual details. No new password needed.

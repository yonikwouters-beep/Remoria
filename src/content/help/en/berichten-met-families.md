## Who can see the conversation?

Only the **managers of the memorial** and **your funeral home**. Guests and other members of the memorial never see this conversation. In your team, the portal administrator, managers and staff members can write. People with view-only access can read along but not write.

## Your inbox

Choose **Messages** in the menu. There you see all conversations with families, newest at the top. New messages also appear via the bell (**Notifications**) at the top. For a new message you also get an email, at most one per hour per conversation, so you're not flooded.

## Starting a conversation yourself

Go to **Memorials**, open the memorial's menu and start the conversation there. For example to ask the family which photos can go in the slideshow or on the memorial card.

## Receiving photos

The family can attach photos from the memorial, for example for the slideshow or the memorial card. You download them in **full quality**: one by one, or all at once with **Download (ZIP)**.

## How the family sends you something

- Via **Funeral home** in the menu of their memorial, where the conversation with you lives.
- From **Media**: they select photos and choose **To funeral home**, optionally with a short message.

## How long does a conversation stay open?

A conversation closes automatically **30 days after the memorial was created**, because the funeral has usually taken place by then. Both sides can still read it, but no longer write. Still something to sort out? Choose **Reopen (30 days)** in the conversation. You can also close a conversation earlier with **Close conversation**.

## Keeping your inbox tidy

Conversation finished? Choose **Hide**. With **Hide read** you tidy away all read conversations at once. You'll find them under the **Hidden** tab. If the family sends a new message, the conversation automatically returns to your inbox.

## Turning messages on or off

Go to **Settings → Messages with families**. When it's off, families don't see this feature; you can still read old conversations. Only the portal administrator or a manager can change this setting. It's on by default.

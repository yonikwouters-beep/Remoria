## What is a memorial?

A memorial is a private, safe place to keep and share memories of a loved one together: photos, videos, stories and tributes. Only people who are invited or whose access is approved can get in.

## Logging in or getting access

You get in through a personal email invitation, a QR code on the funeral card or a shared link. No account yet? You create one in a single step: choose a password or sign in with Google. Already have an account? Just log in with your usual details.

## Finding your way

At the bottom of the screen (on a phone) or in the sidebar (on a computer) you'll find:

- **Home**: the overview and latest memories.
- **Media**: all photos, videos and albums.
- **Tributes**: personal messages.
- **Community Chat**: remembering together.
- **Photo Album**: order a book to keep (optional).

## More than one memorial?

If you have access to several memorials, switch between them via your **profile icon** at the top right, or via **Settings → My memorials**.

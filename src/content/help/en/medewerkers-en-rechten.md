## Can I add team members to my portal?

Yes. Go to **Team members** and invite a colleague with their email address. They receive an invitation email and log in with their own account. You give each team member a role that decides what they can do.

## Roles

- **Portal administrator**: the owner of the portal, with all permissions.
- **Manager**: the same permissions as the administrator, for day-to-day management. Manages memorials and team members.
- **Staff member**: works with memorials and families, without team management or figures.
- **Viewer**: can see everything, but can't change or download anything.

## Changing permissions or removing access

In **Team members** you change someone's role or remove their access. The change applies as soon as that person opens the portal again.

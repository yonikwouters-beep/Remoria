## What is it?

If your memorial runs through a funeral home, its managers can send a message directly to that funeral home. Handy for questions or for passing on photos for the slideshow or the memorial card.

## Who can see the conversation?

Only the managers of the memorial and the funeral home. Guests and other members don't see this conversation.

## Sending a message

Choose **Funeral home** in the menu. Write your message and, if you like, add photos from the memorial with **Add photos**.

## Sending photos from Media

Select the photos in **Media** and choose **To funeral home**. You can add a short message, for example: "These are the photos for the slideshow, the first one can go on the memorial card." The funeral home receives the photos in full quality.

## How long can I send messages?

The conversation stays open for about a month, around the funeral. After that you can still read it. If you have a question later, please contact the funeral home directly.

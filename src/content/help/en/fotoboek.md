## What is the photo book?

With the photos in the memorial you can put together a printed photo book. It gives you a lasting, tangible keepsake to treasure yourself or share with family. You create the book yourself in the memorial and order it right there.

## How do I put a photo book together?

First you choose a theme, then you add your photos. There are four themes:

- **Blank page**: a completely free layout, where you decide every page.
- **Timeless monument**: one strong photo per page.
- **Memory collage**: several photos per page, ideal for a large collection.
- **The life story**: photos combined with your own words.

You need at least 20 photos. While you work, you see a preview of the book.

Tip: are some photos a little blurry or small? Sharpen them first with the [AI-wallet](/help/go/ai-wallet?lang=en) for a nicer print.

## What does a photo book cost?

The price depends on your choices, such as the type of book and the number of pages. While you work, you always see the current price, so you know exactly where you stand before you order.

## How do I order and pay?

Happy with your design? Go to the order step and pay securely online. Your photo book is then printed and delivered to your home.

## Can I save my design and continue later?

Yes. Your work is saved as you go, so you can pick up where you left off. Take all the time you need for a beautiful book.

## Remoria in short?

Remoria is an online place where family and friends keep and share memories of someone who has passed away: photos, videos and stories, in a personal and private memorial.

## Do I need to install an app?

No. Remoria simply works in your browser, on a phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to install.

## Which languages is Remoria available in?

Remoria works in Dutch, French and English. When you create a memorial, you also choose the language of the emails, so they suit the people you invite.

## My question isn't listed.

Ask the assistant in the chat. It knows all the help articles and can help you straight away. If it can't find the answer, it puts you in touch with a team member and passes on your question.
